FIELD MANUAL / BATTLE BASICS

How to play

01 / Choose

Select two different animals from the field guide. Their stats and abilities are unique.

02 / Take turns

Player One goes first. After choosing an ability, the turn passes to the other player.

03 / Attack

Attack power combines the move's base damage and the creature's attack stat. The opponent's defense reduces the hit.

04 / Defend

A defense move braces against the next incoming attack, reducing its damage. It does not deal damage itself.

05 / Win

Reduce the opponent's health to zero to win the match. Reset to start another battle.

06 / Prototype note

This is a local browser prototype. Collections, accounts, online matchmaking, and Discord sync are not connected yet.
